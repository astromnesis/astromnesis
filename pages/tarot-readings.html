Tarot Readings

A tarot reading is a divination method that derives a specific message from a spread of cards: it's a means of seeing into the unknown by interpreting the archetypal meaning of the tarot. The unique order the cards are drawn is used to make sense of the different significations they withhold. Each card represents a spiritual principle and whichever card is drawn provides new insight about your specific story. This can be used to tune into the major energies playing out in your life, as well as to obtain new answers to understand which direction to take. Whether it is love, work or family matters, you're free to open up and ask about the things that are intriguing you the most. This type of divination can unveil a deeper spiritual layer and open up the path to self-awareness.
