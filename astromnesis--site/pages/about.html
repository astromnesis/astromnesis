About Me

Rita Vie is an astrologer and cartomancer born in Porto (Portugal). Her work includes Astrology and Tarot consultations, as well as writing, video and other creative work.

Her interest in astrology and spirituality was deep-seated from an early age, a skill she eventually pursued and formally developed in her mid-twenties under the guidance of an astrology teacher. This learning opportunity marked the turning point from being a mere enthusiast to venturing into professional practice. Since then, Rita Vie's dedication to astrology has only deepened. Over time, astrology went beyond its practical use and permeated her daily life, becoming an integral part of her life-style and how she perceives the world at large.
